# CINTEX Manufacturing Process Optimization

## Project Overview

This project focused on analyzing and improving manufacturing processes within a textile maquila company (anonymized as "CINTEX" for confidentiality purposes).

The objective was to identify operational inefficiencies, analyze production workflows, design performance indicators, and propose automation opportunities using AI-driven solutions.

---

## Business Context

CINTEX operates in a manufacturing environment where production efficiency, quality control, traceability, and timely information flow are critical for operational success.

To support continuous improvement initiatives, a comprehensive analysis of production and administrative processes was conducted.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze existing manufacturing processes
- Identify operational bottlenecks
- Design and evaluate KPIs
- Improve data visibility and reporting
- Propose automation opportunities
- Explore AI applications for operational efficiency

---

## Methodology

- Business Process Analysis
- Manufacturing Process Mapping
- Bottleneck Identification
- KPI Design
- Root Cause Analysis
- Digital Transformation Assessment
- AI Automation Evaluation

---

## Current Challenges

Key issues identified during the analysis included:

- Manual administrative tasks
- Information delays
- Limited process visibility
- Production bottlenecks
- Inefficient data management
- Repetitive operational activities

---

## Proposed Solution

The project proposed a combination of:

- KPI-driven decision making
- Automated data collection
- AI-assisted document processing
- Workflow automation
- Improved reporting and traceability
- Integration with enterprise systems

---

## Dashboard & Analytics

The proposed solution included the definition and monitoring of operational indicators such as:

- Production growth rate

- Packaging time
- Operational efficiency metrics
- Process performance indicators
